# Rattle

The website for Rattle, an iPhone list app that Apple's on-device model can rearrange for you. GitHub Pages serves it from `docs/`.

## Privacy policy

Rattle collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your lists are stored as files on your iPhone and never leave it, unless you share or export one yourself.

The assistant runs on Apple's on-device model. What you ask it, and the list it reads to answer, are processed on your iPhone and aren't sent to us or anyone else.

Rattle has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
