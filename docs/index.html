Rattles

The list that listens.

A list you can talk to

Rattles is a list for your iPhone: groceries, errands, things to remember. You tap, swipe and drag it like Reminders.

Some changes take a dozen taps but only a sentence to say. For those, ask Apple's on-device model, and it rearranges the list for you: group these by aisle, split Dairy into Cheese and Spreads, move the batteries under Hardware, I bought the milk.

What it does

  • Groups and subitems. Any item can hold others. Tick a group to tick everything in it.
  • Undo for every request. If the assistant gets it wrong, one tap puts the list back.
  • Fifteen languages. Ask in your own language, and the reply comes back in it.
  • Export to Markdown. Share any list as a plain checklist.
  • Works without the assistant. Adding, ticking, reordering and deleting never need the model.

Requirements

The assistant uses Apple Intelligence and needs an iPhone that supports it, with Apple Intelligence turned on. On any other iPhone, Rattles is still a complete list app.

Privacy policy

Rattles collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your lists are stored as files on your iPhone and never leave it, unless you share or export one yourself.

The assistant runs on Apple's on-device model. What you ask it, and the list it reads to answer, are processed on your iPhone and aren't sent to us or anyone else.

Rattles has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
